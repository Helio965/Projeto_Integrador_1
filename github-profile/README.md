## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm **Hélio** — a **Brazilian Air Force serviceman with 4 years of service**, currently in my **4th semester of Systems Analysis & Development (ADS) at CEUB**, in Asa Norte, Brasília.

I'm highly **proactive** and **deeply curious about technology** in general — I enjoy taking an idea from zero to a working system. Most of my hands-on work is in **Python**, building backends, automations and AI-assisted tools.

**What I'm building right now:**

- **🧾 [Nexo — Faturamento Inteligente](https://github.com/Helio965/Nexo-Faturamento-Inteligente)** — an intelligent invoicing / business-analytics SaaS. `Python · HTML`
- **🤖 Kernel** — a local multi-agent AI orchestrator with specialized agents (backend, frontend, database, review) wired to a local LLM runtime. `Python · Flask` *(private/local project)*
- **🌱 [Connectagro](https://github.com/Helio965/Connectagro)** — a web platform for agricultural management: crops, inputs, finances, team, map and AI support. `Python`
